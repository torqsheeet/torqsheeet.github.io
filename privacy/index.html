Torqsheet

Privacy policy

Last updated 5 November 2026.

Torqsheet makes small Excel budgeting spreadsheets and free budgeting pages. It is run by one person in Lithuania (EU), who is responsible for the data described here. This page covers this website, our shop listings, the Torqsheet Pinterest app and the Torqsheet YouTube app.

This website

  • No cookies, no analytics or tracking scripts, no forms and no accounts.
  • The calculators run entirely in your browser. Numbers you type never leave your device.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address to run and secure its service; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Links to our products carry tags such as utm_source=torqsheet-site, so the shop can tell us which page a visit came from. They contain nothing about you.

Purchases and free downloads

Products are sold and delivered through Gumroad and Etsy. They take payment and handle your details under their own privacy policies. As the seller we can see what those platforms show sellers (for example the buyer's name, email address and order). We leave that data on the platform: we do not copy it elsewhere, and we do not send marketing emails unless you have agreed to receive them.

The Torqsheet Pinterest app

The Torqsheet Pinterest app is our own publishing tool. It connects only one Pinterest account: Torqsheet's own business account. It is not offered to other Pinterest users.

  • Sign-in: it connects through Pinterest's own OAuth consent screen. We never see, ask for or store a Pinterest password.
  • Permissions requested: boards:read, boards:write, pins:read, pins:write and user_accounts:read.
  • What it does: creates pins and boards on our own account (images of our own products and free pages, linking to them), reads our own pins and boards, and reads the analytics for our own pins (impressions, saves, pin clicks and outbound clicks).
  • What it stores: the access and refresh tokens Pinterest issues, in a file on the owner's computer, outside any code repository, and the analytics totals for our own pins, so we can see which pins are useful. Nothing is shared with or sold to anyone.
  • What it does not do: it does not collect data about other Pinterest users, follow, message, comment, or run ads.
  • Automation: pins are prepared and scheduled by an automated AI assistant that works for Torqsheet under rules set by the owner. It acts only on Torqsheet's own accounts.
  • Disconnecting: the app can be disconnected at any time in Pinterest's settings. When it is disconnected, we delete the stored tokens.

The Torqsheet YouTube app (Torqsheet Publisher)

Torqsheet Publisher is our own tool for the Torqsheet YouTube channel. It uses YouTube API Services and connects only one channel: Torqsheet's own. It is not offered to other people. By watching our videos you are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

  • Sign-in: it connects through Google's own OAuth consent screen. We never see, ask for or store a Google password.
  • Permissions requested: uploading videos to our channel (youtube.upload), reading our own channel and videos (youtube.readonly) and reading our own channel's analytics (yt-analytics.readonly).
  • What it does: uploads short videos we made to our own channel, sets their titles and descriptions, and reads view counts and similar totals for our own videos.
  • What it stores: the access and refresh tokens Google issues, in a file on the owner's computer, outside any code repository, and the totals for our own videos. It does not collect data about viewers or other YouTube users, and nothing is shared with or sold to anyone. Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
  • Automation: videos are scripted and assembled by an automated AI assistant that works for Torqsheet under rules set by the owner, and each video says so.
  • Disconnecting: access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. When it is revoked, we delete the stored tokens.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what personal data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Outside the Gumroad and Etsy order records described above, we hold none. For any privacy question, open an issue at github.com/torqsheeet/torqsheeet.github.io/issues (please do not post personal details there; we will reply with a private way to continue). You can also complain to the Lithuanian State Data Protection Inspectorate.

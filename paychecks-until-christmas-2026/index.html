How many paychecks until Christmas 2026?

Christmas Day 2026 is a Friday. If you want the gift money in hand before you shop, the useful number is not the days left but the paydays left, because that is how many chances you have to set money aside. Enter your next payday and the calculator counts them and splits your goal across them.


Runs in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere. Weekend and holiday date shifts are not applied: if your payroll moves a payday, check that date yourself.

Biweekly Friday paydays, November to Christmas 2026

Most biweekly payrolls that pay on Fridays are on one of two patterns. Find a payday you know in this table to see which one is yours.

PatternPaydays from 1 November 2026Before 20 Dec
A (also paid Fri 1 Jan 2027)6 Nov, 20 Nov, 4 Dec, 18 Dec4
B13 Nov, 27 Nov, 11 Dec, 25 Dec3

Pattern B's last payday falls on Christmas Day itself. Because 25 December is a bank holiday, many payrolls pay the working day before (Thursday 24 December), which is late for shopping. If you are on pattern B, plan on three paydays, not four. Pattern A is the one that gets 27 paychecks in 2027.

Making the number work

  • Move it on payday. Set the per-paycheck amount aside the day you are paid (a separate savings account or envelope), before ordinary spending gets to it.
  • If the amount is too high, lower the goal now rather than finishing on a credit card in January. Gifts, food and travel are usually the three lines to trim.
  • Next year, start in January. The same goal over 26 biweekly paydays is a much smaller amount each time. That is a sinking fund: a yearly cost saved for a bit at a time.

This is general budgeting information, not financial advice.

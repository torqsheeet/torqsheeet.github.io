Three-paycheck months in 2027

If you are paid every two weeks, you get 26 paychecks a year, but most budgets are built on 12 months. That leaves two months (sometimes three) where a third paycheck lands. Knowing which ones in advance lets you budget each month on two paychecks and decide now what the extra ones are for.

The 27-paycheck year

January 1, 2027 is a Friday. If your Friday paydays fall on January 1, the 27th one lands on December 31, so 2027 holds 27 paychecks instead of 26. If your pay is a fixed annual salary split into 26 paychecks, ask your employer how they handle the extra one. January 1 is also a public holiday in many countries, and employers often pay on the working day before, which would move that paycheck into December 2026. The table uses the plain 14-day dates and does not shift for holidays or weekends.

What to do with a third paycheck

A common approach: build the monthly budget on two paychecks, so fixed bills never depend on the third. Then give each extra paycheck a job before it arrives, written into your budget: a sinking fund (holidays, car repairs, annual subscriptions), an extra debt payment, or savings. This is general budgeting information, not financial advice.

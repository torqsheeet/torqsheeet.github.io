Budgeting by payday

Plan your money one paycheck at a time.

Most budgets think in months. If you are paid every week or every two weeks, your bills do not line up that neatly. These free calculators work in paydays: what to set aside from each one, which months bring a third paycheck, and how fast a debt goes.

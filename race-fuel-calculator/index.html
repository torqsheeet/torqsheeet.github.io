Race fuel calculator

How much fuel to put in for a race: enough to finish with a margin, not so much that you carry dead weight for the whole stint. Enter the race length, your lap time and what one lap uses. Works for lap races and timed races, in litres, kilograms, gallons or whatever unit your car shows.

Runs in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere. The example numbers are made up.

How it is worked out

  • Lap race: race laps, plus the formation lap if ticked, times fuel per lap.
  • Timed race: when the clock runs out you still finish the lap you are on, so the laps are the race time divided by your lap time, rounded down, plus one. If you are lapped you will do fewer; if the leader is faster than you, or the rules add a lap, you may do one more, so keep at least one lap of margin.
  • Margin: extra laps of fuel on top, for a fuel-hungry lap in traffic, a safety car or a cool-down lap if your series needs one.
  • Stops: if the total is more than the tank holds, the calculator splits the race into equal stints and says how much each stint needs. It does not count fuel used in the pit lane or the time a stop costs.

The figure is only as good as the fuel per lap you enter. Measure it over a few clean laps at race pace, not on a qualifying lap or a lift-and-coast lap, and measure it again if the weather, the track or the car's engine map changes.
